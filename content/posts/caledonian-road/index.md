Caledonian Road is a novel by Andrew O'Hagan, set in London just after the Covid-19 pandemic. While the narrative centres mostly on Cambell Flynn, a disillusioned academic, the novel is a state of the nation address with a large cast of characters all peripherally connected to him. This retinue includes Russian oligarchs and their wayward sons, Polish drug dealers, grime rappers, elite London fashion houses, art world grifters, old money, new money, and student named Milo with a point to proove. If you feel like the world is spiralling into complexity and chaos, this might be the novel for you. Or maybe not.
